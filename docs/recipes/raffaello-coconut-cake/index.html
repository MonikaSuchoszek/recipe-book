Raffaello Coconut Cake

Four layers of sponge with a light coconut and white chocolate cream, inspired by Raffaello sweets.

  • make-ahead

Portions

Makes 1 cake

using a 20 cm round tin

Ingredients

Sponge

  • 1 sponge cakefrom 5 eggs, see the Sponge Cake recipe

Coconut cream

  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 400 ml cream (30%)cold
  • 2 tbsp icing sugarheaped
  • 60 g desiccated coconutplus extra to decorate
  • 8 gelatine sheets
  • 100 g white chocolate

Layers and soaking

  • blueberry jamor fresh strawberries, sliced
  • coconut liqueuror water with lemon juice and a splash of spirit
  • sugar pearlsto decorate

Method

  1. Bake the Sponge Cake in a 20 cm tin, let it cool and cut it into 4 layers.
  2. Soak the gelatine sheets in cold water, then squeeze them out and dissolve them over a water bath. Let cool slightly.
  3. Whisk the coconut milk with a mixer until airy, then mix in the cooled gelatine. Chill in the fridge for about 1 hour, until it starts to set.
  4. Meanwhile, blitz the desiccated coconut in a blender until fine. Melt the white chocolate and let it cool a little.
  5. When the coconut mixture starts to set, whip the cream with the icing sugar. Add the setting coconut mixture and fold in gently, then fold in the ground coconut and the melted white chocolate.
  6. Soak each layer with coconut liqueur (or water with lemon and sugar), or spread it with raspberry jam instead of soaking. Cover with cream and, if you like, slices of strawberry, then add the next layer.
  7. Spread the rest of the cream around the sides and sprinkle with coconut and sugar pearls. Chill before serving.

Tips

Soak the gelatine in cold water for about 10 minutes to absorb the water. Squeeze water out, microwave for 5 seconds at a time on low power, stirring between each interval, until the gelatin has completely dissolved and the liquid is clear. Be careful not to let it boil, as excessive heat can affect its setting ability. If needed, let it cool slightly before adding it to your mixture, but don't allow it to set.
