Fig and Walnut Muffins

Spiced chocolate muffins with ground almonds, flaxseed, dried figs, walnuts and dark chocolate.

  • vegetarian
  • lunchbox

Portions

Makes 12 muffins

Ingredients

Dry

  • 170 g flour
  • 95 g ground almonds
  • 3 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 50 g brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 10 g baking powder
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • ½ tsp ground ginger
  • ½ tsp ground cardamom
  • 1 pinch salt

Wet

  • 250 ml milk
  • 80 ml vegetable oil

Mix-ins

  • 50 g walnutschopped
  • 75 g dried figschopped
  • 50 g dark chocolatechopped

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C and line a muffin tin with paper cases.
  2. Mix all the dry ingredients.
  3. Add the milk and oil and mix, then stir in the walnuts, figs and chocolate.
  4. Divide over the tin, sprinkle with flaked almonds if you like, and bake for 25–30 minutes, until a skewer comes out dry.
