Salted Caramel Sauce

Smooth caramel sauce from just sugar and cream, with an optional pinch of salt.

  • vegetarian
  • gluten-free
  • quick

Portions

Makes 1 jar

makes about 250 ml

Ingredients

  • 120 g sugar
  • 200 g cream30% fat
  • ½ tsp saltoptional

Method

  1. Tip the sugar into a dry saucepan and spread it out evenly. Heat on medium heat without stirring.
  2. As soon as the sugar has melted and turned amber, pour in a third of the cream and stir.
  3. Add the rest of the cream and cook on low heat for 2–3 minutes, stirring constantly. Stir in the salt, if using.
