1. Peel the potatoes, dice them and cook them in salted water until tender.
2. Cut the bacon into small pieces and fry for a few minutes, then add the sausage sliced into rounds and fry for a few minutes more.
3. Add the bacon and sausage to the broth and bring to the boil.
4. Shake the starter well and pour it all in. The soup will thicken after a few minutes.
5. Season with marjoram, garlic powder, salt, pepper and the cream, and add the cooked potatoes.
