1. Blend the cottage cheese, spinach and eggs until smooth.
2. Add the oil, sugar, flour and baking powder and blend briefly into a thick batter.
3. Heat and lightly grease the waffle iron, then bake the waffles until golden.

## Tips

- These burn quickly, so keep an eye on them and take them out as soon as they're golden.
