1. Put the water and butter in a pan and heat until the butter has fully melted and the mixture comes to the boil.
2. Take the pan off the heat, tip in the flour and stir vigorously with a wooden spoon.
3. Return to the lowest heat and cook briefly, stirring all the time, until the dough comes away from the sides of the pan.
4. Transfer to a bowl and let cool. While it's still slightly warm, beat in the eggs one at a time with a wooden spoon, making sure the dough is smooth before adding the next.
5. Pipe onto a baking tray with a piping bag and bake at 200 °C for 25–30 minutes.

## Tips

- Don't pipe the buns in advance and leave them on the tray, as they dry out. Pipe them just before baking.
