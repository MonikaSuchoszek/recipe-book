1. Mix the ricotta with the egg yolk, sugar and milk.
2. Add the flour mixed with the baking powder and mix well.
3. Whisk the egg white with salt to stiff peaks and fold it in gently.
4. Fry spoonfuls of batter in a lightly oiled pan over medium heat for 2–3 minutes per side, until golden.

## Tips

- Perfect to prepare ahead of time, still soft next day.
