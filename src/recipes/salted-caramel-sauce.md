---
title: Salted Caramel Sauce
description: Smooth caramel sauce from just sugar and cream, with an optional pinch of salt.
date: 2026-10-12
categories: [Sauce & Dressing]
tags: [vegetarian, gluten-free, quick]
image: salted-caramel-sauce.jpg
imageCaption: Poured over an apple semolina cake
servings:
  amount: 1
  unit: jars
  unitSingular: jar
  note: makes about 250 ml
ingredients:
  - name: sugar
    amount: 120
    unit: g
  - name: cream
    amount: 200
    unit: g
    note: 30% fat
  - name: salt
    amount: 0.5
    unit: tsp
    note: optional
---

1. Tip the sugar into a dry saucepan and spread it out evenly. Heat on medium heat without stirring.
2. As soon as the sugar has melted and turned amber, pour in a third of the cream and stir.
3. Add the rest of the cream and cook on low heat for 2–3 minutes, stirring constantly. Stir in the salt, if using.
