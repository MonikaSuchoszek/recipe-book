1. The day before, rinse the split peas until the water runs clear, then soak them overnight. Cook in fresh water for about 50 minutes, until soft.
2. Rinse the sauerkraut, squeeze it out and chop it. Put it in a pot with water, the bay leaves and allspice and cook until soft. Drain.
3. Finely dice the onion and fry it in oil over medium heat for about 15 minutes, until golden.
4. Mix the sauerkraut, peas and onion together and season with pepper.
