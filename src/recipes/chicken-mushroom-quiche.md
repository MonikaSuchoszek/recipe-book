1. Cut the butter into small cubes. Add it to the flour along with the salt and water and combine into a dough. Line the tin and chill for 30 minutes.
2. Fry the chicken in a pan with the mushrooms, onion and garlic until cooked through. Let cool.
3. Whisk the eggs with the cream and season with nutmeg, marjoram, salt and pepper.
4. Pre-bake the pastry at 180 °C for 15–20 minutes.
5. Spread the filling over the pastry, pour over the egg mixture and bake for another 30 minutes.
6. Lay the camembert slices on top 5 minutes before the end and let them melt.

## Tips

- How much egg mixture you need depends on the size of the tin; for a bigger tin add an extra egg and more cream.
