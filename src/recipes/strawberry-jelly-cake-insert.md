1. Sprinkle the gelatine over the water and leave it to bloom. 
2. Blend the strawberries and stir in the icing sugar and lemon juice. Squeeze water out from soaked gelatine and add to warm fruit mix, wait until gelatine fully dissolves but do not boil! Let it cool down.
3. Once the mixture is cold, pour it onto a flat plate or into the base of a tin about 2 cm smaller than your cake.
4. Chill until set (or freeze, which makes it easier to handle), then place it between the cake layers.

## Tips

- Keep the jelly about 2 cm smaller than the cake: the frosting won't stick to the jelly, so the cream needs a border to hold onto the sponge.
