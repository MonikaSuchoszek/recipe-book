    - name: pesto
    - name: toppings of your choice
      note: e.g. mozzarella, tomatoes, olives
---

1. Mix the starter ingredients in a big bowl and leave for 10 minutes.
2. Grate the raw cauliflower finely, then add the remaining dough ingredients to the starter and mix. The dough will be sticky.
3. Cover and leave to rise in a warm place for about 30 minutes.
4. Using tablespoon and wet hands (the dough stays sticky), shape small pizzas directly on a lined baking tray.
5. Pre-bake at 200 °C for 5 minutes.
6. Spread the pizzas with pesto, add the toppings and bake for another 15 minutes (fan is fine for last 3-5min to make it crispy).

## Tips

- They keep in the fridge; reheat them in a covered pan.
