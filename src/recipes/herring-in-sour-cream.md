    amount: 1

  - name: spring onion
    amount: 1
    unit: handful

  - name: black pepper

---

1. Drain the herring and pat dry. If it's very salty, soak it in cold water or milk for 30 minutes first. Cut into bite-sized pieces.
2. Peel and core the apple and cut it into small cubes. Finely chop the onion.
3. Mix the sour cream with the mayonnaise and season with pepper.
4. Gently fold in the herring, apple, onion and most of the spring onion.
5. Chill for at least 1 hour and sprinkle with the rest of the spring onion before serving.
