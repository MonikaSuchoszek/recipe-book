1. Make sure all ingredients are at room temperature. Preheat the oven to 180 °C and line a muffin tin with paper cases.
2. Weigh the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt into a bowl and mix. Add the butter and rub it in with your fingers until crumbly.
3. In a separate bowl, mix the yoghurt with the egg, extra egg white and vanilla.
4. Add the wet ingredients to the dry and mix with a whisk. A few small lumps of butter are fine.
5. Fill the cases three quarters full and bake for 18–20 minutes, until a skewer comes out dry. Don't bake them any longer or they'll dry out.

## Tips

- Great to serve with cream on the top
