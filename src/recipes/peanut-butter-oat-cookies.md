---
title: Peanut Butter Oat Cookies
description: Chewy honey-sweetened oat cookies with peanut butter, plus dark chocolate or cranberries if you like.
date: 2026-10-08
categories: [Snack, Baking]
tags: [vegetarian, quick, lunchbox]
servings:
  amount: 12
  unit: cookies
  unitSingular: cookie
ingredients:
  - name: rolled oats
    amount: 1.5
    unit: cup
  - name: peanut butter
    amount: 4
    unit: tbsp
    note: up to 5 for richer cookies
  - name: honey
    amount: 2
    unit: tbsp
    note: up to 3 for sweeter cookies
  - name: baking soda
    amount: 1
    unit: tsp
  - name: egg
    amount: 1
  - name: salt
    amount: 1
    unit: pinch
    scale: false
  - name: dark chocolate or dried cranberries
    note: optional, chopped
---

1. Preheat the oven to 170 °C and line a baking tray with baking paper.
2. Mix all the ingredients into a sticky dough, folding in the chocolate or cranberries last.
3. Shape into balls with wet hands, place them on the tray and flatten slightly.
4. Bake for about 10 minutes, then let them firm up on the tray before moving them.
