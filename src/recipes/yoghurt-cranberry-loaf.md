1. Whisk the eggs with the sugar and vanilla sugar for about 5 minutes, until thick.
2. Melt the white chocolate into the warm melted butter.
3. Add the yoghurt, butter and chocolate, lemon zest, and the flour mixed with the baking powder. Mix just until combined, then fold in the cranberries.
4. Line a small loaf tin with baking paper, pour in the batter and bake at 180–190 °C for about 40 minutes. Let cool.
5. For the glaze, break the chocolate into small pieces, pour the hot milk over it, stir until smooth and pour over the cold cake.
