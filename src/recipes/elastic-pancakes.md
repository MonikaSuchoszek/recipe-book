1. Put all the ingredients in a tall jug and blend with a hand blender into a smooth, lump-free batter.
2. Heat a non-stick frying pan over medium heat.
3. Pour in a thin layer of batter and swirl the pan so it covers the bottom.
4. Cook until the edges lift and the underside is golden, then flip and cook the other side briefly.

## Tips

- A hand blender makes the batter smooth in seconds; whisking by hand works too, but takes longer to get rid of the lumps.
