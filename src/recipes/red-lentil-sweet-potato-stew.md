1. Cook the sweet potatoes until soft and mash them.
2. Heat some oil in a large pot and cook the onion, garlic, bell pepper and spices for 5 minutes (add a splash of water if needed).
3. Add the lentils, diced tomatoes, tomato paste and broth. Bring to the boil, then simmer for 10–15 minutes.
4. Add the peas and coconut milk and cook until heated through.
5. Stir in the mashed sweet potato.
6. Serve with bread or rice and a spoonful of Greek yoghurt on top.

## Tips

- To speed up the recipy, freeze portions of mashed sweet potatoes
