---
title: Carnival Layered Cheesecake
description: Striped cheesecake (torcik karnawałowy) with alternating coconut and dulce de leche layers, cocoa and a hint of blackcurrant.
date: 2026-10-08
categories: [Cake]
tags: [vegetarian, make-ahead]
servings:
  amount: 1
  unit: cakes
  unitSingular: cake
  note: using a 26 cm springform tin
ingredients:
  - name: farmer's cheese (twaróg)
    amount: 1
    unit: kg
    note: ground
  - name: potato starch
    amount: 3
    unit: tbsp
  - name: icing sugar
    amount: 60
    unit: g
  - name: eggs
    amount: 5
  - name: amaretto
    amount: 2
    unit: tbsp
  - name: coconut milk
    amount: 200
    unit: ml
  - name: dulce de leche (masa kajmakowa)
    amount: 1
    unit: can
  - name: blackcurrant jam
    note: for a thin layer
  - name: cocoa powder
    note: for dusting
---

1. Mix the cheese with the potato starch and icing sugar briefly on low speed. Add the eggs one at a time, then the amaretto, and mix until smooth.
2. Divide the mixture in half. Mix the coconut milk into one half (white) and the dulce de leche into the other (caramel).
3. Pour half of the white mixture into the tin and bake at 180 °C for 13–15 minutes.
4. Pour over half of the caramel mixture and bake again for 13–15 minutes, until set.
5. Dust with cocoa, pour over the rest of the white mixture and bake for another 13–15 minutes.
6. Spread a thin layer of jam over the top, pour over the rest of the caramel mixture and bake for a final 13–15 minutes.
7. Turn off the oven and let the cake cool slowly with the door ajar. Chill overnight.
8. Dust with cocoa before serving.
