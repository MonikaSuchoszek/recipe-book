1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks (don't overbeat!).
2. Add the sugar in portions, then the egg yolks, whisking continuously.
3. Sift in the flour and fold in very gently with a spatula.
4. Line the cake tin with baking paper (bottom only). Don't grease the sides.
5. Bake at 160-170 °C for 35-40 minutes.
6. Drop the hot cake (in its tin) onto the floor from about 40 cm.
7. Only cut the cake away from the sides of the tin once it has cooled.

## Tips

- The original recipe uses a Polish glass (szklanka, about 250 ml): ¾ glass sugar, ¾ glass flour and ¼ glass potato starch or cocoa. Flour and starch together weigh 180 g.
