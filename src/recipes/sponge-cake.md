---
title: Sponge Cake
description: Classic light Polish sponge, made with only eggs, sugar, flour and potato starch.
date: 2026-09-26
categories: [Baking, Dessert]
tags: [vegetarian, dairy-free]
servings:
  amount: 1
  unit: cakes
  unitSingular: cake
  note: using a 20 cm diameter round tray
ingredients:
  - name: eggs
    amount: 5
  - name: sugar
    amount: 180
    unit: g
  - name: wheat flour
    amount: 135
    unit: g
  - name: potato starch
    amount: 45
    unit: g
    note: or cocoa for a chocolate sponge
---

1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks (don't overbeat!).
2. Add the sugar in portions, whisking continuously at high speed, then the egg yolks while whisking at low speed.
3. Sift in the flour and potato starch in a few portions and fold in very gently with a spatula.
4. Line the cake tin with baking paper (bottom only). Don't grease the sides.
5. Bake at 170 °C for 35–40 minutes.
6. Drop the hot cake (in its tin) onto the floor from about 40 cm.
7. Only cut the cake away from the sides of the tin once it has cooled.
