1. Put the sugar, the zest of all the lemons and the squeezed juice in a heatproof bowl. Mix and heat over a water bath until the sugar has dissolved.
2. Add the eggs and stir vigorously. Keep cooking and stirring until the curd thickens (even 30 minutes).
3. Press it through a sieve, then immediately stir in the butter until melted.
4. Pour into a clean jar, let cool and keep in the fridge.

## Tips

- Store your lemon curd in the fridge for 2–3 weeks. Enjoy it with pancakes, porridge, or waffles, or simply stir a spoonful into natural yoghurt for a delicious, lemony treat!
