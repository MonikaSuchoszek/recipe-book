  Dressing:

      amount: 5

    - name: sugar
      amount: 2

      amount: 3
      unit: tbsp
---

1. Cut the broccoli into florets, cook for 5 minutes, then let cool.
2. Fry the diced bacon in a dry pan (no fat).
3. Toast the sunflower seeds in a pan for 3 minutes.
4. Chop the red onion and the pear or apple, then combine everything.
5. Add the chopped walnuts.
6. In a small pot, heat the vinegar with the sugar. Let it cool, then stir in the mayonnaise.
7. Toss the salad with the dressing.
