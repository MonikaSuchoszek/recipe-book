---
title: Apple Shortcrust Cake
description: Polish jabłecznik, with a thick layer of soft cinnamon apples between two layers of grated shortcrust.
date: 2026-10-08
categories: [Cake, Baking]
tags: [vegetarian, autumn]
servings:
  amount: 1
  unit: cakes
  unitSingular: cake
  note: using a baking tray of about 20 × 30 cm
ingredients:
  Shortcrust:
    - name: wheat flour
      amount: 320
      unit: g
    - name: butter
      amount: 250
      unit: g
      note: cold
    - name: sugar
      amount: 5
      unit: tbsp
    - name: baking powder
      amount: 1.5
      unit: tsp
    - name: vanilla sugar
      amount: 16
      unit: g
    - name: egg
      amount: 1
  Apples:
    - name: apples
      amount: 1.5
      unit: kg
    - name: sugar
      amount: 5
      unit: tbsp
    - name: cinnamon
      amount: 1
      unit: tsp
---

1. Mix the dry ingredients, rub in the butter, then add the egg last and knead into a dough.
2. Divide the dough in two, wrap and put both halves in the freezer for about 1 hour.
3. Peel the apples, cut them into small pieces and cook them in a pan with the sugar and cinnamon for about 20 minutes, until they start to fall apart. Let cool.
4. Grate one half of the dough over the bottom of a lined tray, spread the apples on top and grate the second half over the apples.
5. Bake at 180 °C for 50 minutes.
