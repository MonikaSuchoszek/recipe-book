---
title: Quick Bread (Chleb szybki)
description: No-knead overnight loaf with a crackling crust, baked in a covered dish.
date: 2026-09-26
categories: [Baking]
tags: [vegan, dairy-free, make-ahead]
servings:
  amount: 1
  unit: loaves
  unitSingular: loaf
ingredients:
  - name: flour
    amount: 600
    unit: g
  - name: dry yeast
    amount: 7
    unit: g
  - name: sugar
    amount: 1
    unit: tbsp
  - name: salt
    amount: 1
    unit: tbsp
  - name: olive oil
    amount: 2
    unit: tbsp
  - name: warm water
    amount: 500
    unit: ml
---

1. Mix the dry ingredients in a large bowl.
2. Add the warm water and oil, and mix well into a dough.
3. Cover with cling film and leave in the fridge overnight (about 10 h).
4. In the morning, let it rest for 30 minutes at room temperature.
5. Dust the counter with flour, knead the dough briefly but vigorously and shape it into a loaf (fairly loosely).
6. Put an ovenproof dish with a lid into the oven and heat to 250 °C.
7. Take out the dish and place the loaf inside.
8. Bake covered at 220 °C for 30 minutes, then another 20 minutes uncovered.
