1. Mix the flour, sugar, oats, baking powder and baking soda in a bowl.
2. Add the oil, yoghurt and eggs (at room temperature) and stir everything together with a spoon. Add a few drops of vanilla if you like.
3. Pour into the tin, arrange the fruit on top and press it in deeply. Sprinkle with coconut if using.
4. Bake at 180 °C for about 45 minutes.

## Tips

- For a higher-protein version, use 220 g flour and 30 g protein powder instead of 250 g flour.
