1. Sift the flour, cocoa, icing sugar and baking powder together and mix. Add the cold butter and water and knead into a dough.
2. Prepare the tin. Preheat the oven to 180°C (conventional, no fan). Grease a ceramic baking tin with butter and dust it with flour or semolina, shaking off any excess. Press the dough evenly over the bottom and about 2 cm up the sides of the tin. Place in the fridge while you prepare the filling.
3. Briefly mix the cheese with the sugar, custard powder and vanilla sugar.
4. Melt the milk, coffee and chocolate together in a small pan over low heat. Let cool completely. Add to the cheese and mix briefly, then add the egg and mix only until combined.
5. Pour the filling over the prepared base and bake for about 40 minutes. The edges should be set, while the centre may still have a gentle, jelly-like wobble when you carefully move the tin. Don't worry if the middle still looks slightly loose; this is normal, and the cheesecake will continue to set as it cools.
6. Allow the cheesecake to cool completely (the best overnight) before decorating with whipped cream and coffee beans, if desired.
