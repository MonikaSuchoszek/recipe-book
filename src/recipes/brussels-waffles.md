1. Mix the egg yolks with the yeast and milk, stirring until the yeast has dissolved.
2. Add the water and sift in the flour, then mix until there are no lumps.
3. Melt the butter, let it cool and stir it into the batter.
4. Whisk the egg whites with salt to stiff peaks and fold them in gently with a spatula, keeping in as much air as possible.
5. Leave the batter to rise for 20 minutes, then bake in a hot waffle iron until golden (3-4 minutes).
