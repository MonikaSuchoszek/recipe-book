---
title: Shortcrust Pastry for a Tart (Kruche ciasto na tartę)
description: Crisp, lightly sweet tart base with just five ingredients.
date: 2026-09-26
categories: [Baking]
tags: [vegetarian]
servings:
  amount: 1
  unit: tart bases
  unitSingular: tart base
ingredients:
  - name: wheat flour
    amount: 200
    unit: g
  - name: cold butter
    amount: 120
    unit: g
  - name: powdered sugar
    amount: 50
    unit: g
  - name: salt
    amount: 1
    unit: pinch
    scale: false
  - name: cold water
    amount: 2
    unit: tbsp
    note: up to 3 tbsp, as needed
---

1. Cut the butter into small cubes.
2. Add it to the flour along with the remaining ingredients and combine into a dough.
3. Chill in the fridge for 30 minutes.
4. Bake at 200 °C for 20 minutes.
