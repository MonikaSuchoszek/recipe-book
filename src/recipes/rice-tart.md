1. Mix the warm milk with the sugar and yeast and leave for 5 minutes, until the yeast starts to foam.
2. Add the melted butter, egg yolk, sifted flour and salt. Knead for a few minutes, until the dough no longer sticks.
3. Cover the bowl with cling film and leave in a warm, draught-free place for about 1 hour, until doubled in size.
4. Roll out the dough to the shape of the tin and line the tin with it. Prick it all over with a fork, cover with cling film again and leave to rise for 20 minutes.
5. Meanwhile, bring the water to the boil with the salt, lemon zest and cinnamon stick. Add the rice and cook on low heat until the water is almost completely absorbed, about 15 minutes.
6. Add the milk, cream and sugar and simmer on low heat for 30 minutes. Remove the lemon zest and cinnamon.
7. In a separate bowl, whisk the eggs with the potato starch. Stir in a little of the hot rice, then pour the mixture back into the pot, stirring constantly, until it starts to thicken like a pudding. Cook for a few more minutes until thick.
8. Let the filling cool slightly, spread it over the dough and bake at 180 °C for 35–40 minutes.
9. Chill in the fridge overnight so the tart sets properly.
