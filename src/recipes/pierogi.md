1. Sift the flour into a bowl, add the salt and pour in the boiling water. Mix with a spoon until it forms a ball.
2. Knead on a floured worktop for a full 8 minutes, until smooth and elastic. Wrap in cling film and leave for 30 minutes.
3. For the potato filling: cook the potatoes and mash them thoroughly while still warm. Fry the bacon and add it to the potatoes, then fry the onion, add it too and season with pepper.
4. For the sweet cheese filling: blend all the ingredients until smooth.
5. Roll the dough out thinly (2–3 mm) and cut out circles with a 7 cm glass. Put a teaspoon of filling on each, fold over and pinch the edges firmly closed.
6. Cook in a large pot of salted boiling water for 3 minutes from the moment they float to the surface.

## Tips

- For a smaller batch of about 30 pierogi, use 300 g flour and 250 ml boiling water. The sweet cheese filling as written is the right amount for that smaller batch.
