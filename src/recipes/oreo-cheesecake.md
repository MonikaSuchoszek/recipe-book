1. Cover the dates with boiling water and leave for 15 minutes. Drain, then blend them with the oats, cocoa and coconut oil into a thick, smooth paste.
2. Press the paste evenly into the bottom of a springform tin lined with baking paper.
3. Soak the gelatine in cold water for about 10 minutes, squeeze it out, dissolve it over a water bath and let it cool briefly.
4. Mix the cheese with the icing sugar, vanilla sugar, yoghurt and stevia.
5. Stir a few spoonfuls of the cheese mixture into the gelatine one by one, then pour it all back into the cheese mixture and mix and add crumbled oreos.
6. Pour onto the base and chill overnight.
7. Dust with cocoa before serving or decorate with havles of Oreo cookies.

## Tips
Soak the gelatine in cold water for about 10 minutes to absorb the water. Squeeze water out, Microwave for 5 seconds at a time on low power, stirring between each interval, until the gelatin has completely dissolved and the liquid is clear. Be careful not to let it boil, as excessive heat can affect its setting ability. If needed, let it cool slightly before adding it to your mixture, but don't allow it to set.

Layered variant: skip the date base and layer the Oreo cheese mixture between layers of chocolate sponge cake, like a layered sponge cake.
