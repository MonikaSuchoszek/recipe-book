---
title: Savoury Muffins
description: Cheesy muffins with olives, sun-dried tomatoes or whatever mix-ins you have at hand.
date: 2026-09-26
categories: [Baking, Snack]
tags: [vegetarian, lunchbox]
servings:
  amount: 12
  unit: muffins
  unitSingular: muffin
  note: Estimated for a standard 12-hole muffin tin; the original recipe doesn't state a yield.
ingredients:
  Batter:
    - name: flour
      amount: 300
      unit: g
    - name: baking powder
      amount: 1.5
      unit: tsp
    - name: baking soda
      amount: 0.5
      unit: tsp
    - name: salt
      amount: 0.5
      unit: tsp
    - name: egg
      amount: 1
    - name: milk
      amount: 250
      unit: ml
    - name: yoghurt
      amount: 60
      unit: g
    - name: oil
      amount: 60
      unit: ml
    - name: garlic
      amount: 1
      unit: clove
  Add-ins:
    - name: cheddar
      amount: 200
      unit: g
      note: grated
    - name: mix-ins
      amount: 500
      unit: ml
      note: about 2 glasses, e.g. halved olives, spring onion, sun-dried tomatoes, capers from a jar, leek
---

1. Combine the ingredients, mixing only until they come together (don't overmix).
2. Bake at 180 °C for 25 minutes.
