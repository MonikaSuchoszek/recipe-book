categories: [Baking, Snack]
tags: [vegetarian, lunchbox]
image: savoury-muffins.jpg
imageCaption: A plate of freshly baked savoury muffins
gallery:
  - file: closeup.jpg
    caption: Close-up of the mix-ins
servings:
  amount: 18
  unit: muffins
  unitSingular: muffin
ingredients:
  Batter:
    - name: flour
      amount: 300
      unit: g
    - name: baking powder
      amount: 1.5
      unit: tsp
    - name: baking soda
      amount: 0.5
      unit: tsp
    - name: salt
      amount: 0.5
      unit: tsp
    - name: egg
      amount: 1
    - name: milk
      amount: 250
      unit: ml
    - name: yoghurt
      amount: 60
      unit: g
    - name: oil
      amount: 60
      unit: ml
    - name: garlic
      amount: 1
      unit: clove
      note: finely grated
  Mix-ins:
    - name: mozzarella
      amount: 100
      unit: g
      note: grated
    - name: mix-ins
      amount: 2
      unit: cup
      note: e.g. halved olives, spring onion, sun-dried tomatoes, capers from a jar, leek
---

1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C and line a muffin tin with paper/silicon cases.
2. Mix the batter ingredients until just combined (don't overmix).
3. Gently fold in the mozzarella and mix-ins.
4. Divide over the tin and bake for about 30 minutes, until golden.
