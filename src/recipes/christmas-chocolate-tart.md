---
title: Christmas Chocolate Tart
description: Cocoa and gingerbread-spiced shortcrust with a prune and cranberry layer under a silky chocolate ganache.
date: 2026-10-08
categories: [Cake]
tags: [vegetarian, make-ahead, winter, Christmas]
servings:
  amount: 1
  unit: tarts
  unitSingular: tart
  note: using a 22 cm tart tin
ingredients:
  Spiced shortcrust:
    - name: wheat flour
      amount: 150
      unit: g
    - name: cocoa powder
      amount: 1
      unit: tbsp
    - name: gingerbread spice
      amount: 1
      unit: tbsp
    - name: cold butter
      amount: 90
      unit: g
    - name: powdered sugar
      amount: 40
      unit: g
    - name: salt
      amount: 1
      unit: pinch
      scale: false
    - name: cold water
      amount: 1.5
      unit: tbsp
  Fruit layer:
    - name: prunes
      amount: 6
    - name: dried cranberries
      amount: 1
      unit: handful
  Chocolate cream:
    - name: chocolate
      amount: 250
      unit: g
      note: dark and milk, to taste
    - name: cream (30%)
      amount: 150
      unit: ml
    - name: amaretto
      amount: 1
      unit: tbsp
      note: or almond extract
  To decorate:
    - name: chocolate shavings
    - name: edible gold dust
      note: optional
---

1. Cut the butter into small cubes. Add it to the flour along with the cocoa, gingerbread spice, sugar, salt and water and combine into a dough. Line the tart tin, chill for 30 minutes and bake at 200 °C for about 20 minutes. Let cool completely.
2. Cover the prunes and cranberries with boiling water and leave for 10 minutes. Drain, blend to a purée and spread over the cooled base.
3. Chop the chocolate finely (for example 150 g dark and 100 g milk chocolate). Heat the cream until almost boiling, pour it over the chocolate, add the amaretto and stir until smooth.
4. Pour the chocolate cream over the fruit layer.
5. Decorate with chocolate shavings and gold dust, then chill for at least 6 hours to set.
