1. Crumble the yeast into the flour, sprinkle with the sugar and pour over the warm milk. Leave for 10 minutes.
2. Add the whole eggs and mix with a spoon until the dough forms a ball. Add the melted butter and knead for at least 10 minutes into a soft dough using standing mixer.
3. Cover and leave in warm place to rise for 45 minutes to 1 hour.
4. Divide the dough into 3 parts and roll each one out into a circle of the same size.
5. Place the first circle on a lined baking tray and spread it with half of the poppy seed filling. Cover with the second circle, spread with the rest of the filling and top with the last circle.
6. Put a small glass in the centre and cut the dough into 16 strips from the glass to the edge, leaving the centre whole. Twist pairs of neighbouring strips away from each other and pinch their ends together to form the points of the star.
7. Brush with beaten egg and bake at 180 °C for about 25 minutes, until golden.

## Tips

- Can be also done in a shape of a Christmas tree.
