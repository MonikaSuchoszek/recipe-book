---
title: Gingerbread Cookies
description: Crisp, buttery honey cookies with gingerbread spice and cinnamon, perfect for Christmas.
date: 2026-10-08
categories: [Baking, Snack]
tags: [vegetarian, winter, Christmas]
servings:
  amount: 40
  unit: cookies
  unitSingular: cookie
  note: depends on the size of your cookie cutters
ingredients:
  - name: wheat flour
    amount: 320
    unit: g
  - name: baking soda
    amount: 0.5
    unit: tsp
  - name: gingerbread spice
    amount: 1
    unit: tsp
    note: up to 2 for spicier cookies
  - name: cinnamon
    amount: 1
    unit: tsp
  - name: icing sugar
    amount: 80
    unit: g
  - name: egg
    amount: 1
  - name: cold butter
    amount: 170
    unit: g
  - name: honey
    amount: 1
    unit: tbsp
    note: up to 2 for sweeter cookies
---

1. Sift the flour, baking soda and icing sugar into a bowl and add the spices.
2. Chop the butter into the flour with a knife, then add the egg and honey and knead into a dough.
3. Chill in the fridge for 30 minutes.
4. Roll out to 4 mm thick, cut out cookies and bake at 175 °C for 7–8 minutes.
