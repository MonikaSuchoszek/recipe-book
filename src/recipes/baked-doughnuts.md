---
title: Baked Doughnuts
description: Soft, buttery yeast doughnuts (pączki) baked instead of fried, with a chocolate spread centre.
date: 2026-10-08
categories: [Baking]
tags: [vegetarian]
servings:
  amount: 16
  unit: doughnuts
  unitSingular: doughnut
  note: using a 7.5 cm glass to cut the circles
ingredients:
  Dough:
    - name: dry yeast
      amount: 10
      unit: g
    - name: milk
      amount: 100
      unit: ml
      note: warm
    - name: wheat flour
      amount: 370
      unit: g
    - name: sugar
      amount: 50
      unit: g
    - name: salt
      amount: 0.5
      unit: tsp
    - name: eggs
      amount: 3
    - name: butter
      amount: 170
      unit: g
      note: soft
  Filling and glaze:
    - name: chocolate spread
      amount: 16
      unit: tsp
    - name: egg
      amount: 1
      note: beaten, for brushing
---

1. Mix the warm milk with the yeast and leave for 5 minutes, until foamy.
2. Sift the flour into a bowl and add the sugar and salt. Pour in the yeast mixture, add the eggs and start kneading.
3. Add the butter a little at a time and knead for 10–15 minutes, until the dough is smooth and no longer very sticky (it will still be quite soft).
4. Cover and leave to rise for 1 hour.
5. Gently roll the dough out to 1 cm thick and cut out circles with a glass of about 7.5 cm.
6. Put a teaspoon of chocolate spread on each circle, seal the edges well and place seam side down on baking paper.
7. Switch the oven on at its lowest temperature and let the doughnuts rise inside until puffy.
8. Take them out, heat the oven to 180 °C, brush with beaten egg and bake for 15 minutes.
