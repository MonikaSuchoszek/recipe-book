title: Easter Forest Moss Cake

  amount: 1

ingredients:
  Sponge:
    - name: fresh spinach

      unit: g

      amount: 220

      unit: tsp

      unit: g

      amount: 4
      unit: tbsp

      amount: 1

      amount: 2

    - name: strawberries, raspberries and pomegranate seeds
---

1. Heat the spinach in a pan until wilted, then blend it to a very smooth purée.
2. Whisk the eggs with the sugar for 5–7 minutes. Pour in the oil slowly while still whisking, then add the spinach purée.
3. Sift in the flour with the baking powder and mix only briefly.
4. Pour into a tray lined with baking paper and bake at 180 °C for about 30 minutes. Let cool.
5. Slice off the top (about a third of the cake) and crumble it into fine "moss".
6. Whip the cream with sugar (about 3/4 of the way to stiff peaks) without overbeating. Then add the mascarpone and mix shortly with a mixer, then add lemon zest and lemon curd and mix again very briefly.
7. Spread the cream over the cake and scatter the crumbled moss on top.
8. Tuck the fruit in between the moss.
