---
title: Banana Breakfast Waffles
description: Egg-free buttermilk-style waffles with banana, cinnamon and vanilla.
date: 2026-10-08
categories: [Breakfast]
tags: [vegetarian, quick]
servings:
  amount: 7
  unit: waffles
  unitSingular: waffle
  note: About 800 kcal for the whole batch. Might vary depending on the size of your waffle iron.
ingredients:
  - name: milk
    amount: 280
    unit: ml
  - name: apple cider vinegar
    amount: 10
    unit: ml
  - name: wheat flour
    amount: 200
    unit: g
  - name: banana
    amount: 60
    unit: g
    note: half a large banana, mashed
  - name: baking powder
    amount: 1
    unit: tsp
  - name: cinnamon
    amount: 1
    unit: tsp
  - name: vanilla extract
    note: to taste
  - name: salt
    amount: 0.5
    unit: tsp
  - name: oil
    note: for the waffle iron
---

1. Mix the milk with the vinegar and leave for 5 minutes.
2. Add the remaining ingredients and mix into a smooth batter.
3. Bake in a hot, lightly oiled waffle iron until golden.
