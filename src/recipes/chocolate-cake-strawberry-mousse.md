1. Heat the butter, chocolate, water and amaretto until melted, then let cool.
2. Whisk the egg yolks with half the sugar until fluffy. Whisk the whites with the rest of the sugar until thick and glossy.
3. Gently fold the cooled chocolate into the yolks, then the flour, and finally the whites.
4. Bake at 160 °C for 30 minutes. The cake will sink and be dense; that's how it should be. Let cool completely.
5. Blend the strawberries with the lemon juice and icing sugar, then heat in a pan. Add the jelly and stir until dissolved, without boiling. Let cool until mixture reaches 30C.
6. Whip the cold cream to stiff peaks and fold in the cooled strawberry mixture.
7. Spread the mousse over the cold chocolate base and chill overnight.
8. Decorate with halved strawberries.

## Tips

- Instead of the packet of strawberry jelly you can use 10 g gelatine soaked in cold water for 10minutes.
