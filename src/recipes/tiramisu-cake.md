1. Bake the [Sponge Cake](../sponge-cake/) in a 20 cm tin, let it cool and cut it into 3 layers.
2. Whisk the egg yolks with the sugar for a few minutes until pale and fluffy.
3. Add the mascarpone and mix on low speed just until combined.
4. Whip the cream and fold it in, then add amaretto to taste.
5. Soak each sponge layer well with the coffee and amaretto and layer with the cream.
6. Cover the sides with cream and press the ladyfingers against them. Dust the top generously with cocoa.
7. Chill overnight.

## Tips

- For a classic tiramisu dessert instead of a cake, replace the whipped cream with the 3 egg whites whisked to stiff peaks and folded in gently.
