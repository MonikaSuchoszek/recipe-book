---
title: Apple Cheesecake
description: Polish sernik with cinnamon apples on a shortcrust base, finished with a grated crust on top.
date: 2026-10-08
categories: [Cake]
tags: [vegetarian, autumn]
servings:
  amount: 1
  unit: cakes
  unitSingular: cake
  note: using a 26 cm springform tin
ingredients:
  Shortcrust:
    - name: wheat flour
      amount: 250
      unit: g
    - name: icing sugar
      amount: 70
      unit: g
    - name: baking powder
      amount: 1
      unit: tsp
    - name: butter
      amount: 160
      unit: g
      note: cold
    - name: egg
      amount: 1
    - name: salt
      amount: 1
      unit: pinch
      scale: false
  Cheese filling:
    - name: twaróg (farmer's cheese)
      amount: 1
      unit: kg
      note: triple-ground
    - name: vanilla custard powder (budyń)
      amount: 40
      unit: g
      note: one packet
    - name: sugar
      amount: 100
      unit: g
    - name: vanilla sugar
      amount: 16
      unit: g
    - name: eggs
      amount: 2
  Apples:
    - name: apples
      amount: 500
      unit: g
      note: peeled and diced
    - name: sugar
      amount: 3
      unit: tbsp
    - name: cinnamon
      amount: 2
      unit: tsp
    - name: icing sugar
      note: for dusting
---

1. Knead the shortcrust ingredients into a dough. Roll out two thirds of it and line the bottom and sides of the tin; put the remaining third in the freezer. Chill the lined tin in the fridge.
2. Mix the cheese with the custard powder, sugar, vanilla sugar and eggs until smooth.
3. Toss the apples with the sugar and cinnamon.
4. Pour the cheese filling onto the dough, spread the apples over it and grate the frozen dough on top.
5. Bake at 180 °C for 60 minutes. Let cool and dust with icing sugar.
